My game is a hybrid physical/digital game in which small robotic tanks battle each other on a real tabletop battlefield.  

The robots are 3d printed, with custom ESP32 based electronics, motorised tracks and turrets, infrared shooting and hit detection and cameras for a first person view from each tank.  The tanks are about 13cm×8cm.  

What sets this game apart from other IR tank battle games is the gameplay.  I'm building a Unity app which will act as a server, running the turn based gameplay, keeping track of action points, shields and hit points.
